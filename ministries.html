Ministries

Every member has a place to serve. Here's where the work of this congregation happens, week to week.

01

Youth & Children

Bible classes and mentorship for every age, from cradle roll through the teen years.

02

Benevolence

Practical help for our neighbors in Harlem — food, clothing, and a listening ear.

03

Visitation

Visiting the sick, the homebound, and the grieving — carrying the church to those who can't come to us.

04

Evangelism

Bible correspondence courses, personal studies, and community outreach across the city.

05

Worship & Song Leading

Training voices and hearts to lead the congregation in a cappella praise.

06

Hospitality

Welcoming visitors, hosting fellowship meals, and making Harlem Church of Christ feel like home.
