Who We Are

About & Leadership

We are not a denomination. We are simply Christians, seeking to be the church you can read about in the New Testament — nothing more, nothing less.

Our Story

Rooted in Harlem, built on Scripture.

Harlem Church of Christ exists to be exactly what the New Testament describes: a local body of baptized believers, gathering to worship God in spirit and truth, encourage one another, and carry the gospel to our city.

We wear no name but Christ's, follow no creed but the Bible, and welcome every soul who seeks Him — regardless of background, history, or how long it's been since they walked through a church door.

What We Believe

Simple convictions, plainly stated.

I.

The Scriptures

The Bible is God's complete and sufficient word — our only rule of faith and practice.

II.

The Church

Christ builds one church, His body, and adds the saved to it — not to a denomination.

III.

Salvation

We are saved by grace through faith, expressed in repentance and baptism into Christ.

IV.

Worship

We gather each Lord's Day to sing, pray, study the Word, and share the Lord's Supper.

V.

Fellowship

Every member has a place to serve and a family to belong to — this is not a spectator faith.

VI.

Mission

We exist as much for Harlem as for ourselves — sent to love our neighbors and share Christ.

Leadership

Shepherds & servants.

Our elders and ministers are devoted to teaching, prayer, and the care of this congregation.

Elders

Our elders shepherd the flock in love, keeping watch over the church as those who will give account (Hebrews 13:17).

Deacons

Our deacons serve the practical needs of the congregation, freeing the church to grow in ministry.

Evangelist

Our preaching minister devotes himself to the Word and to teaching, in season and out.
