How Do I Get Saved?

"Unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God." Here is what the Bible says that looks like.

Step 1

Hear the Gospel

Faith comes by hearing the word of Christ (Romans 10:17). It starts with learning who Jesus is and what He has done.

Step 2

Believe

Believe that Jesus is the Son of God, that He died for your sins, and that God raised Him from the dead (John 8:24, Romans 10:9).

Step 3

Repent

Turn from sin toward God — a change of heart that leads to a changed life (Acts 3:19, Luke 13:3).

Step 4

Confess

Openly confess Jesus as Lord before others (Romans 10:9-10, Matthew 10:32).

Step 5

Be Baptized

Be immersed in water for the forgiveness of your sins, raised to walk in newness of life (Acts 2:38, Romans 6:3-4).

Step 6

Walk Faithfully

Continue in the Word, in fellowship, and in service — being born again is a beginning, not an end (Acts 2:42).

Ready to take this step — or just have questions?

We would be honored to study the Bible with you, answer your questions, or simply pray with you. There's no wrong way to start this conversation.
